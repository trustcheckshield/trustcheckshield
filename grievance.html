Response time
We will acknowledge and respond to your complaint within 24–48 hours of receiving it.

What you can contact us about

  • Your personal data – access, correction, deletion, or withdrawing consent.
  • A report you want removed or corrected – for example one that is false, or names you or your business.
  • Abuse or unlawful content in the app.
  • Any other complaint about the service.

How to write to us

Use the subject line Grievance - Trust Check Shield and include:

  1. Your name and the email address you use in the app (if you have an account).
  2. What the issue is and what you want us to do.
  3. For a report you want removed or corrected: the exact link, phone number or UPI ID, the date you saw it, and any proof that it is wrong (for example, official documents showing the number or site is yours).

We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a data request.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your complaint within 24–48 hours.
  2. We review it and may contact you for more information.
  3. We tell you our decision and the action taken. If a full resolution needs longer, we will tell you the expected timeline.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India or another competent authority, as provided under applicable law.

Delete your account and reports

  • In the app (fastest): sign in, open Profile, tap Delete my account & reports, and type DELETE to confirm. Your account and the reports linked to it are removed. This cannot be undone.
  • By email: if you cannot sign in, or you submitted a report without signing in, write to us as described above and give the exact item you reported.

Data stored only on your own device (history, language, local copies) is cleared when you delete your account here or clear your browser data.

Children

The app is for people aged 18 and over. If you believe a child has used it or given us personal data, email us and we will act promptly. See Children in the Privacy Policy.
