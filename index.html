Report Fraud

If you lost money or shared a code, act fast. Quick reports give the best chance of stopping a transfer.

  1. Call your bank on the number printed on your card and ask them to block the card, UPI or account.
  2. Call 1930, the national cyber crime helpline, or file a report at cybercrime.gov.in.
  3. Report suspicious calls, SMS and WhatsApp numbers on the Sanchar Saathi portal.
  4. Keep evidence: screenshots, phone numbers, UPI IDs, links and transaction IDs. Do not delete the chat.
  5. Block and report the sender inside the app you received it on.

How does TrustCheck Shield work?

It looks for known scam patterns in the text you give it, using rules that run in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded.

Does Low Risk mean something is safe?

No. Low Risk only means no common warning signs were found in the text. New scams can look clean, so stay careful.

Why was a real site or message flagged?

The rules are strict on purpose. Words such as login, verify and free are often used in scams, so they raise the risk even on honest pages. Check the sender or site another way.

What if I already clicked or shared an OTP?

Call your bank right away using the number on your card, and block the card or UPI. Change your passwords. Then report the fraud to the national helpline 1930 or at cybercrime.gov.in.

How do I report a scam message or number?

Block and report the sender inside the app (WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, your SMS app or your email service). For fraud involving money, call 1930 or use cybercrime.gov.in.

Why is a short link or a download flagged?

Short links such as bit.ly hide the real address, and APK, EXE, SCR and ZIP files can carry malware. A file named EICAR is an antivirus test file, harmless by design, but it is flagged on purpose.

What are color prediction and gaming reward scams?

Messages promising welcome gifts or bonuses from apps such as 91Club, then asking you to log in, recharge or pay a fee to withdraw. The winnings never arrive. Do not deposit money or share bank details.
