3. How we use your data

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To run community checks and display scam reports.
  • To prevent spam, abuse and false reports.
  • To respond to your requests and complaints, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or make decisions about you based on profiling. The risk score you see is produced by pattern checks that run in your browser.

4. Consent and your choices

On your first visit we ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older and that you accept this policy and our Terms. Until you accept, sign-in, community checks and reporting stay switched off. You can withdraw consent at any time using Privacy choices at the bottom of the app, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing does not affect processing that already happened.

5. Who we share data with

We use these service providers (“processors”) to run the app:

  • Supabase – database and sign-in. Our project is hosted in the Mumbai, India region.
  • Google (Gmail) – delivers the one-time code emails to your inbox.
  • Vercel and GitHub – host the website files.

They process data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose data if the law requires it or to protect users from fraud or abuse.

6. Reports are public

Reports you submit can be read by every user of the app. They may include details about third parties, for example a phone number reported as a scam. If you believe a report about you is wrong or harmful, use the dispute process on the Grievance Officer page.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data – until you delete your account.
  • Reports linked to your account – until you delete them (deleting your account removes them) or we remove them under our Terms or the law.
  • Reports submitted without signing in – cannot be linked to a person. Ask the Grievance Officer to remove one by giving us the exact item you reported.
  • Server logs – for the period set by each provider.
  • Data on your device – until you clear your browser data or delete your account.

  • Delete your account and linked reports: in the app go to Profile → Delete my account & reports. This is permanent.
  • Any other request: email pritam44760@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge within 24–48 hours.
